# Warranty

## How long is the frame warranty?
Every Brightside frame has a lifetime warranty for the original owner against manufacturing defects.

## What about parts and components?
Components such as brakes, gears and wheels are covered for 2 years from the date of purchase.

## Are e-bike batteries covered?
E-bike batteries are covered for 3 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## How do I make a warranty claim?
Email warranty@brightside.example with your order number and photos of the problem. We reply within 2 business days.

# Returns and refunds

## How long do I have to return something?
You can return any item within 45 days of delivery, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging.

## How do I start a return?
Open your order on brightside.example/orders and choose "Start a return". We email you a prepaid return label.

## Who pays for return shipping?
Return shipping is free if the item arrived damaged or defective. For other returns, $9 is deducted from your refund for the label.

## When do I get my refund?
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.

## Can I return a custom-built bike?
No. Custom-built bikes are made to order and cannot be returned, but they are covered by the warranty.

# Shipping

## When will my order ship?
Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern ship the same business day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below $75, standard shipping costs $7.95. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Do you offer express shipping?
Yes. Express shipping costs $24 and delivers in 1 to 2 business days. Express is not available for full bikes, only for parts and accessories.

## Where do you ship?
We ship to the United States and Canada. We do not ship to other countries yet.

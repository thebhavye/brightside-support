# Contact and support

## When is support open?
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 6 pm Eastern. We are closed on Sundays.

## How do I reach a person?
Call 1-800-555-0142 during support hours, or email help@brightside.example any time.

## Do you offer bike fittings?
Yes. A professional bike fitting at our Portland store costs $60, and it is free with any full bike purchase.
